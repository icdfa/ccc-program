# Assignment 1.1: The Consultant's Role - Statement of Work (SOW)

## Assignment Overview

This assignment is designed to assess your understanding of the cybersecurity consultant's role and the consulting lifecycle. You will be required to develop a professional Statement of Work (SOW) for a simulated security assessment engagement. This is a practical exercise that will allow you to apply the concepts learned in Module 1.1 and develop a critical document used in real-world consulting engagements.

## Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this assignment, you will be able to:

-   **Analyze a client's needs** and translate them into a formal project scope.
-   **Develop a comprehensive Statement of Work (SOW)** that clearly defines the scope, deliverables, timeline, and cost of a consulting engagement.
-   **Articulate the value of a security assessment** in a professional and persuasive manner.
-   **Demonstrate an understanding of the key components of a consulting engagement**, including assumptions, client responsibilities, and terms and conditions.

## Scenario

**Client:** "Financially Secure," a mid-sized financial institution with 500 employees and a growing online presence.

**Background:** Financially Secure has recently experienced a minor security incident involving a sophisticated phishing attack that resulted in the compromise of a few employee email accounts. While no customer data was breached, the incident has raised concerns at the executive level about the institution's overall security posture. The CEO, Ms. Evelyn Reed, has engaged you, a senior cybersecurity consultant from "CyberGuard Consulting," to conduct a comprehensive security assessment.

**Initial Discussion Points with the CEO:**

-   The CEO wants to understand the institution's current security posture and its ability to defend against modern cyber threats.
-   She is particularly concerned about compliance with financial regulations, including the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) and the Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act (GLBA).
-   She wants to know if their current security controls are adequate to protect against common cyber threats, such as ransomware, phishing, and insider threats.
-   The institution has a limited budget for the assessment and requires a clear, detailed breakdown of the costs and deliverables.
-   The CEO has also expressed a desire for the assessment to be as non-disruptive to business operations as possible.

## Your Task

Based on the scenario and the initial discussion points, develop a professional Statement of Work (SOW) for the security assessment engagement. The SOW should be comprehensive, well-structured, and tailored to the specific needs of Financially Secure.

## SOW Structure and Guidance

Your SOW should include the following sections. Use the guidance provided to ensure each section is comprehensive and professional.

1.  **Introduction/Overview:** A brief overview of the engagement, including the client's name, the consultant's name (CyberGuard Consulting), and the purpose of the SOW. This section should set the stage for the rest of the document.

2.  **Scope of Work:** A detailed description of the services to be provided. This is the heart of the SOW and should be as specific as possible.
    -   **In-Scope Systems:** Clearly define the specific systems, networks, and applications that will be assessed (e.g., internal network, external-facing web applications, cloud infrastructure).
    -   **Assessment Activities:** Detail the types of assessments that will be conducted (e.g., vulnerability assessment, penetration testing, policy and procedure review, social engineering testing).
    -   **Out-of-Scope Activities:** Explicitly state any activities that are not included in the engagement to avoid scope creep (e.g., remediation of identified vulnerabilities, incident response services).

3.  **Deliverables:** A list of the specific deliverables that will be provided to the client.
    -   **Security Assessment Report:** A detailed report with findings, risk ratings, and actionable recommendations.
    -   **Executive Summary Presentation:** A high-level presentation for the C-suite that summarizes the key findings and recommendations in business terms.
    -   **Technical Debriefing:** A meeting with the client's technical stakeholders to discuss the findings and recommendations in detail.

4.  **Timeline:** A high-level timeline for the engagement, including key milestones and deadlines. A phased approach is recommended (e.g., Phase 1: Planning and Scoping, Phase 2: Assessment, Phase 3: Reporting and Debriefing).

5.  **Assumptions:** A list of any assumptions that you have made in developing the SOW. This helps to clarify the basis for your proposed scope, timeline, and pricing.

6.  **Client Responsibilities:** A list of the client's responsibilities, such as providing access to systems and personnel, providing a dedicated point of contact, and ensuring the availability of relevant documentation.

7.  **Pricing and Payment Terms:** A clear and transparent breakdown of the costs for the engagement. This should include professional fees, any anticipated travel and expenses, and the payment schedule.

8.  **Terms and Conditions:** Standard terms and conditions, such as confidentiality, liability, and termination clauses. You may include a reference to a Master Services Agreement (MSA) if one is in place.

>
> CyberGuard Consulting will perform the following assessment activities:
>
> -   **External Penetration Testing:** An attempt to compromise the external-facing systems from the internet.
> -   **Internal Vulnerability Assessment:** A scan of the internal network to identify known vulnerabilities.
> -   **Review of Policies and Procedures:** A review of the client's existing security policies and procedures against industry best practices.

## Appendix B: Common Pitfalls to Avoid

-   **Vague Scope:** Avoid using vague language in the scope of work. Be as specific as possible about what is and is not included.
-   **Unrealistic Timeline:** Ensure that your timeline is realistic and accounts for potential delays.
-   **Hidden Costs:** Be transparent about all costs, including travel and expenses.
-   **Lack of Client Responsibilities:** Clearly outline what is expected of the client to ensure a smooth engagement.

# Assignment 1.2 : Business Risk Management - Integrated Risk Assessment

## Objective

This assignment is designed to enhance your understanding of business risk management by integrating both qualitative and quantitative risk assessment methodologies. You will conduct a comprehensive risk assessment for a simulated client, perform detailed financial calculations to quantify risk, and provide data-driven recommendations, mirroring the responsibilities of a professional cybersecurity consultant.

## Scenario

**Client:** "Innovate Inc.," a technology startup.

**Background:** Innovate Inc. is preparing to launch a new mobile application called "ConnectSphere." ConnectSphere is a social networking app that allows users to share photos, videos, and personal updates, and includes an in-app purchase feature.

**Your Role:** You are a cybersecurity consultant hired by Innovate Inc. to conduct a comprehensive risk assessment for the ConnectSphere app and provide a justifiable, cost-benefit analysis for your security recommendations.

### Financial and Threat Data

To facilitate a quantitative analysis, Innovate Inc. has provided the following data points:

-   **User Database (Asset):** Valued at **$3,500,000**, considering development costs, user acquisition efforts, and projected future revenue.
-   **In-App Purchase System (Asset):** Valued at **$1,500,000**.
-   **Threat 1: Major Data Breach:** Industry data indicates a **25% annual probability (ARO = 0.25)** of a major data breach for a company of Innovate Inc.'s profile. Such a breach is estimated to impact **40% of the user database's value (EF = 0.4)** due to regulatory fines, customer notification costs, and reputational damage.
-   **Threat 2: Payment Fraud:** A specific vulnerability has been identified in the payment processing module. If exploited, it could lead to fraudulent transactions and financial theft equivalent to **10% of the payment system's value (EF = 0.1)**. This type of vulnerability is exploited, on average, **once every two years (ARO = 0.5)**.
-   **Proposed Control:** The company is considering a comprehensive security solution including a Web Application Firewall (WAF), regular code audits, and employee training. The total annual cost for this solution is **$75,000**.
-   **Control Effectiveness:** The proposed control is projected to reduce the ARO of a major data breach to **0.1** and the ARO of the payment fraud vulnerability to **0.1**.

## Your Task

Your assignment is divided into two parts. You must complete both a qualitative and a quantitative risk assessment.

### Part 1: Qualitative Risk Assessment

Conduct a qualitative risk assessment for the ConnectSphere app. Your assessment should include:

1.  **Asset Identification:** Identify and describe the key assets that need to be protected (e.g., user data, financial data, source code, backend servers).
2.  **Threat Identification:** Identify potential internal/external and malicious/accidental threats to the ConnectSphere app.
3.  **Vulnerability Identification:** Identify potential technical and non-technical vulnerabilities in the app and its supporting infrastructure.
4.  **Risk Analysis:** For at least five identified risks, assess the likelihood and impact using a qualitative scale (e.g., Low, Medium, High).
5.  **Risk Prioritization:** Create a 5x5 risk matrix and plot your identified risks to prioritize them visually.
6.  **Risk Treatment Recommendations:** For your top three high-priority risks, recommend a risk treatment option (Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, or Avoid) and provide a brief justification.

### Part 2: Quantitative Risk Assessment & Exercises

Using the financial and threat data provided, perform the following calculations and exercises. Show your work for each calculation.

1.  **Calculate the Single Loss Expectancy (SLE)** for:
    a.  A major data breach affecting the user database.
    b.  The exploitation of the payment fraud vulnerability.

2.  **Calculate the Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE)** for:
    a.  The risk of a major data breach **before** the proposed control is implemented.
    b.  The risk of payment fraud **before** the proposed control is implemented.
    c.  What is the total current ALE for these two risks combined?

3.  **Perform a Cost-Benefit Analysis** for the proposed security control:
    a.  Calculate the new ALE for both the data breach and payment fraud risks **after** the proposed control is implemented.
    b.  Calculate the **Return on Security Investment (ROSI)** for the proposed control. Use the total ALE before and after the control in your calculation.

4.  **Decision-Making Exercise:** Based on your ROSI calculation, write a brief, one-paragraph recommendation to the CEO of Innovate Inc. explaining whether the $75,000 investment is financially justified.

## Submission Format

Please submit your complete assessment as a single, well-organized PDF file. Use tables to present your findings for both the qualitative and quantitative sections to ensure clarity and readability.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your submission will be evaluated on the following:

-   **Completeness and Thoroughness:** Comprehensive identification of assets, threats, and vulnerabilities.
-   **Qualitative Analysis:** Logical and well-justified qualitative risk analysis and prioritization.
-   **Quantitative Accuracy:** Correctness of your SLE, ALE, and ROSI calculations. All steps in the calculations must be shown.
-   **Clarity and Organization:** The assessment is clearly written, well-structured, and easy to understand.
-   **Actionability and Justification:** Your recommendations are realistic, actionable, and supported by the data from your analysis.

# Assignment 1.4: Foundational Frameworks - NIST CSF Mapping

## Objective

This assignment is designed to assess your understanding of foundational cybersecurity frameworks and your ability to map a client's current security controls to the NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF). You will step into the shoes of a cybersecurity consultant to analyze a real-world scenario and provide actionable recommendations.

---

## The Scenario: HealthFirst Healthcare Network

**Client:** "HealthFirst," a rapidly growing regional healthcare provider.

**The Situation:** 
HealthFirst operates a network of three hospitals and fifteen outpatient clinics. As a healthcare provider, they handle vast amounts of sensitive Electronic Health Records (EHR) and are subject to strict regulatory requirements, including HIPAA. 

Recently, a neighboring hospital system suffered a devastating ransomware attack that crippled their operations for weeks. Spooked by this event, HealthFirst's Chief Information Officer (CIO), Sarah Jenkins, has hired you as an external cybersecurity consultant. 

Sarah is concerned about the organization's ability to protect patient data and maintain critical care services during a cyber event. She has asked you to assess their current security posture against the industry-standard NIST Cybersecurity Framework (CSF) to see where they stand and where they need to improve.

**Current Security Controls (Your Discovery Findings):**
During your initial interviews with the IT team, you discovered the following controls are currently in place:

1.  **Perimeter Defense:** The organization has a robust enterprise firewall in place to protect its network perimeter from external threats.
2.  **Patch Management:** All servers are patched on a monthly schedule during a designated maintenance window.
3.  **Endpoint Protection:** The organization uses standard antivirus software deployed on all employee workstations and laptops.
4.  **Human Firewall:** All employees (from doctors to administrative staff) are required to complete an annual, 30-minute online security awareness training module.
5.  **Data Resilience:** The organization has a daily backup schedule and a documented recovery plan in place for critical databases.

---

## Your Task: The Consultant's Assessment

Based on the scenario and the list of current security controls, your job is to analyze HealthFirst's security posture using the NIST CSF. 

**Step 1: Map the Controls**
Map HealthFirst's current security controls to the five core functions of the NIST Cybersecurity Framework:
*   **Identify**
*   **Protect**
*   **Detect**
*   **Respond**
*   **Recover**

*Note: Some controls may map to more than one function, or some functions may have no controls mapped to them at all.*

**Step 2: Analyze and Recommend**
For each of the five NIST CSF functions, you must provide:
1.  **Current State:** Identify which of the current security controls apply to this function and provide a brief explanation of *how* they apply.
2.  **Gap Analysis:** Identify what is missing. Where is HealthFirst vulnerable within this specific function?
3.  **Consultant's Recommendations:** Provide at least one actionable, realistic recommendation to improve their posture for this function.

## Evaluation Criteria

Your consultant report will be evaluated based on the following criteria:

*   **Accuracy (30%):** Is the mapping of the current security controls to the correct NIST CSF functions accurate and logical?
*   **Comprehensiveness (30%):** Have you identified the critical gaps in the current security controls, especially considering they are a healthcare provider?
*   **Actionability (20%):** Are your recommendations for improvement realistic, specific, and valuable to the client?
*   **Professional Clarity (20%):** Is your mapping clear, concise, well-organized, and presented professionally using the required table format?

# Lab 1.2: Business Risk Management - A Hands-On Consultant's Guide

## 1. Introduction & Learning Objectives

Welcome to this hands-on lab on Business Risk Management. You will assume the role of a senior cybersecurity consultant tasked with conducting a comprehensive risk assessment for a fast-growing e-commerce company, "Go-Green." This lab emphasizes practical application, requiring you to perform both qualitative and quantitative risk analysis using a provided dataset, make strategic recommendations, and communicate risk in financial terms.

### Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this lab, you will be able to:

-   **Utilize a provided dataset** to identify and categorize business and cybersecurity risks.
-   **Conduct qualitative risk analysis** by interpreting likelihood and impact scores.
-   **Perform quantitative risk analysis** by calculating Single Loss Expectancy (SLE), Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE), and Return on Security Investment (ROSI) using given data.
-   **Develop data-driven risk mitigation strategies** and justify them with a cost-benefit analysis.
-   **Communicate risk and recommendations** effectively to an executive audience.

### How to Use This Lab

This lab is structured into sequential parts. Follow each step, complete the tasks using the provided dataset, and document your findings. This is a practical exercise; focus on applying the concepts to the data.

## 4. Part 2: Quantitative Risk Assessment & Financial Analysis

Now, you will translate the abstract risks into concrete financial figures for the Top 5 Critical Risks identified in Part 1.

### Task 2.1: Calculate the Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE) for Your Top 5 Risks

For each of your top 5 critical risks, perform a full quantitative analysis to determine the Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE). Use the `Asset_Value`, `Exposure_Factor`, and `ARO` (Annualized Rate of Occurrence) from the Data Source table.

**Follow these steps for each of the 5 risks:**

1.  **Identify the Asset Value (AV):** Use the `Asset_Value` from the dataset.
2.  **Estimate the Exposure Factor (EF):** Use the `Exposure_Factor` from the dataset.
3.  **Calculate the Single Loss Expectancy (SLE):**
    `SLE = Asset Value (AV) × Exposure Factor (EF)`
4.  **Estimate the Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO):** Use the `ARO` from the dataset.
5.  **Calculate the Annualized Loss Expectancy (ALE):**
    `ALE = Single Loss Expectancy (SLE) × Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO)`

**Action:** Create a table to document your calculations for each of the top 5 risks. After calculating the individual ALEs, sum them to find the **Total Current ALE**.

### Task 2.2: Develop Mitigation Strategies and Calculate ROSI

Assume Go-Green is considering implementing a comprehensive security package to address these top 5 risks. The estimated annual cost for this package is **$500,000**. This package is expected to reduce the ARO for each of your top 5 risks by a certain percentage (e.g., a 50% reduction means new ARO = original ARO * 0.5).

**Action:**

1.  **Propose a Mitigation Strategy:** Briefly describe a general security package (e.g., WAF, data encryption, employee training, API security, endpoint protection) that would address your top 5 risks.
2.  **Estimate Control Effectiveness:** For each of your top 5 risks, assume the proposed security package will reduce the `ARO` by **60%**. Calculate the `New ARO` for each risk.
3.  **Calculate the New ALE:** Recalculate the `ALE` for each of the top 5 risks using the `New ARO`.
4.  **Calculate the Return on Security Investment (ROSI):**
    `ROSI = (Total ALE before controls - Total ALE after controls - Cost of Control) / Cost of Control`

**Action:** Create a table showing the `Original ARO`, `New ARO`, `Original ALE`, `New ALE`, and the `ROSI` calculation for the overall security package.

## 5. Part 3: Executive Presentation & Recommendations

Your analysis is complete. Now you must present your findings to the board of directors in a clear, concise, and compelling manner.

### Task 3.1: Write the Executive Summary

**Action:** Write a **one-page executive summary** (approximately 250-300 words) that includes:

1.  **The Bottom Line Up Front:** Start with your main conclusion, summarizing the current risk and the potential reduction with investment.
2.  **Current Risk Landscape:** Briefly describe the top 5 critical risks and their combined financial impact (Total Current ALE).
3.  **Proposed Mitigation Strategy:** State the proposed investment and its cost.
4.  **The Business Case (ROI):** Clearly state the calculated ROSI and the net financial benefit.
5.  **Key Recommendations:** List the top 3-5 prioritized action items.

### Task 3.2: Create Detailed Risk Treatment Recommendations

For each of your top 5 risks, provide a detailed, actionable recommendation.

**Action:** For each of the top 5 risks, create a "Risk Treatment Card" that includes:

1.  **Risk Description:** A clear, business-focused description.
2.  **Current Financial Impact:** The calculated ALE for that risk.
3.  **Recommended Response:** (Mitigate, Transfer, Accept, or Avoid).
4.  **Specific Controls:** A list of 3-5 specific, tangible security controls or actions to be taken.
5.  **Implementation Timeline:** A realistic, phased timeline (e.g., Month 1, Month 2, etc.).
6.  **Success Metrics (KRIs):** How will you measure the success of the controls? (e.g., "Reduction in successful phishing attempts by 90%.")

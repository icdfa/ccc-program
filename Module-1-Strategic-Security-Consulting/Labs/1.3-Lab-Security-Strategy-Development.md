# Lab 1.3: Security Strategy Development - Roadmap Simulation

**Author: Aminu Idris, AMCPN**

### Objective

This lab will provide you with a practical, hands-on experience in developing a security strategy. You will simulate the process of creating a multi-year security roadmap for a client, applying the concepts from the study material to align security initiatives with specific business goals and risks.

### Scenario

**Client:** "RetailRevolution," a traditional brick-and-mortar retailer.

**Background:** RetailRevolution is a mid-sized, family-owned retailer specializing in home goods with 50 stores across the country. To compete with large online retailers, the company is launching two major digital initiatives: a new e-commerce platform (built on Shopify) and a mobile customer loyalty program. These initiatives are critical for the company's future growth. The company will be processing credit card payments directly on its new platform, making compliance with the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS) a mandatory requirement.

**Current State:** The company has a limited IT budget and a small IT team that has historically focused on operational support. There is no dedicated security personnel. Their current security posture is reactive, with basic firewalls and endpoint antivirus software, but no centralized monitoring, formal security policies, incident response plan, or employee security awareness program.

**Your Role:** You are a cybersecurity consultant hired by RetailRevolution to help them develop a 3-year security roadmap that enables their digital transformation securely.

#### 4. Executive Presentation Outline

Prepare a brief presentation outline of your roadmap. The outline should be targeted at the CEO of RetailRevolution and should focus on the business value and benefits of the proposed security initiatives.

**Required Sections:**

*   **Introduction:** Your role and the purpose of the roadmap.
*   **Understanding Your Business:** Briefly state your understanding of their goals (e-commerce, loyalty program).
*   **Top Security Risks to Your Growth:** Present the highest-priority risks in business terms (e.g., "Risk of customer data theft impacting brand trust").
*   **Our 3-Year Strategic Path to Secure Growth:** Present the roadmap highlights, focusing on major themes for each year.
*   **Business Benefits & Return on Investment:** Explain how the roadmap enables business, builds customer trust, and avoids costly breaches and fines.
*   **Next Steps:** Recommend immediate next steps (e.g., approve budget for Year 1 initiatives).

### Evaluation Criteria

Your submission will be evaluated based on:

*   **Strategic Alignment:** Does your roadmap clearly support the client's business objectives and mitigate key risks?
*   **Quality of Risk Assessment:** Is your identification and prioritization of risks logical and well-justified?
*   **Comprehensiveness and Realism:** Have you proposed a realistic and comprehensive set of initiatives for a company of this size and budget?
*   **Prioritization:** Is your prioritization of initiatives logical, starting with foundational and high-risk items first?
*   **Clarity and Persuasiveness:** Is your submission, especially the presentation outline, clear, concise, and persuasive for a non-technical executive audience?

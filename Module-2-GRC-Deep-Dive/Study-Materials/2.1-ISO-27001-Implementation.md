## 1. Learning Outcomes
By the end of this comprehensive module, you will be able to:
- **Understand** the core philosophy of ISO 27001:2022 and its focus on risk-based thinking.
- **Execute** a 10-step implementation roadmap from initiation to certification.
- **Analyze** and apply all 93 Annex A controls across the four new themes.
- **Develop** critical ISMS documentation, including the Statement of Applicability (SoA) and Risk Treatment Plan (RTP).
- **Prepare** an organization for Stage 1 and Stage 2 certification audits.

## 2. The 10-Step Implementation Roadmap
Implementing an ISO 27001:2022 ISMS is a structured journey. Below is the detailed breakdown of each step.

### Step 1: Secure Management Commitment
Without "buy-in" from the top, the ISMS will fail.
- **Activity:** Present the business case (risk reduction, competitive advantage).
- **Deliverable:** Approved budget and a signed "Information Security Policy."

### Step 2: Define the Context & Scope
What are we protecting, and where are the boundaries?
- **Activity:** Identify internal/external issues (legal, cultural, technical) and interested parties (clients, regulators).
- **Deliverable:** **ISMS Scope Document.**

### Step 3: Leadership & Governance
Assigning who does what.
- **Activity:** Establish an Information Security Steering Committee.
- **Deliverable:** Roles and Responsibilities Matrix (RACI).

### Step 4: Risk Assessment Methodology
How will we identify and rank risks?
- **Activity:** Choose a framework (e.g., ISO 27005 or ISO 31000). Define "Impact" and "Likelihood" scales.
- **Deliverable:** Risk Assessment Methodology Document.

### Step 5: Conduct Risk Assessment
The "Heart" of the ISMS.
- **Activity:** Identify threats to assets, vulnerabilities, and the resulting risk level.
- **Deliverable:** **Risk Register.**

### Step 6: Risk Treatment Plan (RTP)
How do we fix the risks?
- **Activity:** Apply the 4 Ts: **Treat** (Mitigate), **Transfer** (Insurance), **Tolerate** (Accept), or **Terminate** (Avoid).
- **Deliverable:** Risk Treatment Plan.

### Step 7: Statement of Applicability (SoA)
The master list of controls.
- **Activity:** Review all 93 Annex A controls. Mark each as "Applicable" or "Not Applicable" with justification.
- **Deliverable:** **Statement of Applicability (SoA).**

### Step 8: Implement Controls & Training
Putting theory into practice.
- **Activity:** Roll out technical tools (MFA, Encryption) and conduct Security Awareness Training.
- **Deliverable:** Training logs and technical configuration records.

### Step 9: Internal Audit & Management Review
Checking your own work.
- **Activity:** Hire an independent auditor to find gaps. Management reviews the results.
- **Deliverable:** Internal Audit Report.

### Step 10: Certification Audit
The final test.
- **Activity:** Stage 1 (Documentation Review) and Stage 2 (Effectiveness Audit) by a Certification Body.
- **Outcome:** **ISO 27001 Certification.**

## 3. Full Breakdown of Annex A Controls (93 Controls)
The 2022 version organizes controls into **4 Themes**.

### Theme 1: Organizational Controls (37 Controls)
*Focus: Governance, Policies, and Supplier Management.*

| ID | Control Title | Key Implementation Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5.1 | Policies for InfoSec | Must be reviewed annually and approved by management. |
| 5.7 | **Threat Intelligence** | *New:* Collect and analyze data on current attack trends. |
| 5.12 | Classification of Info | Label data as Public, Internal, Confidential, or Secret. |
| 5.23 | **Cloud Services** | *New:* Specific security requirements for SaaS/PaaS/IaaS providers. |
| 5.30 | ICT Readiness for BC | *New:* Ensure IT systems can recover during a disaster. |

### Theme 2: People Controls (8 Controls)
*Focus: Human Resources and Security Awareness.*

| ID | Control Title | Key Implementation Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 6.1 | Screening | Background checks for all new hires and contractors. |
| 6.3 | Awareness & Training | Regular phishing simulations and security briefings. |
| 6.7 | Remote Working | Secure VPNs and home-office security policies. |

### Theme 3: Physical Controls (14 Controls)
*Focus: Facilities and Equipment Security.*

| ID | Control Title | Key Implementation Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 7.1 | Physical Perimeters | Fences, gates, and secure reception areas. |
| 7.4 | **Physical Monitoring** | *New:* Use of CCTV and alarms to detect unauthorized entry. |
| 7.7 | Clear Desk & Screen | No sensitive info left on desks or unlocked screens. |

### Theme 4: Technological Controls (34 Controls)
*Focus: Technical Safeguards and Secure Coding.*

| ID | Control Title | Key Implementation Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 8.1 | User Endpoints | Antivirus, EDR, and disk encryption on laptops. |
| 8.5 | Secure Authentication | Mandatory Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA). |
| 8.11 | **Data Masking** | *New:* Hiding sensitive data (e.g., PII) in test environments. |
| 8.12 | **Data Leakage Prev.** | *New:* Tools to prevent unauthorized data export (DLP). |
| 8.28 | **Secure Coding** | *New:* Security integrated into the software dev lifecycle (SDLC). |

## 4. Deep Dive: The 11 New Controls in 2022
If you are transitioning from the 2013 version, focus heavily on these:
1.  **5.7 Threat Intelligence:** Proactive monitoring of the threat landscape.
2.  **5.23 Cloud Services:** Managing the "Shared Responsibility Model."
3.  **5.30 ICT Readiness:** Business continuity for IT systems.
4.  **7.4 Physical Security Monitoring:** Active surveillance.
5.  **8.9 Configuration Management:** Hardening systems against default settings.
6.  **8.10 Information Deletion:** Securely wiping data when no longer needed.
7.  **8.11 Data Masking:** Protecting PII in non-production environments.
8.  **8.12 Data Leakage Prevention:** Monitoring and blocking data exfiltration.
9.  **8.16 Monitoring Activities:** Logging and analyzing user behavior.
10. **8.23 Web Filtering:** Blocking access to malicious websites.
11. **8.28 Secure Coding:** Principles for writing secure software.

## 5. Risk Assessment Methodology (ISO 27005 vs 31000)
| Feature | ISO 31000 (Enterprise Risk) | ISO 27005 (InfoSec Risk) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Scope** | All business risks (Financial, Strategic). | Specifically Information Security risks. |
| **Approach** | High-level, principles-based. | Detailed, asset-based or process-based. |
| **Best Use** | Corporate governance. | Technical ISMS implementation. |

## 6. Knowledge Check & Self-Assessment
1.  **Scenario:** A company uses AWS for all its data. Which new 2022 control is most relevant?
    *   *(Answer: 5.23 Information security for use of cloud services.)*
2.  **True or False:** The Statement of Applicability (SoA) only lists the controls you HAVE implemented.
    *   *(Answer: False. It must list all 93 controls and justify why any were excluded.)*
3.  **What is the "Shared Responsibility Model" in cloud security?**
    *   *(Answer: The provider secures the infrastructure; the customer secures the data and access.)*

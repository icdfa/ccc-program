## Assignment 4.1: Executive Communication and Reporting - Group Board Presentation

### Objective

This assignment is designed to assess your ability to communicate complex technical findings to a non-technical audience. You will be required to prepare and deliver a 10-minute group presentation to a simulated Board of Directors on the state of security and proposed investments.

### Scenario

**Client:** "Manufacturing Co.," a large manufacturing company specializing in automotive parts and industrial equipment.

#### Organization Overview

**Company Profile:**
- **Headquarters:** Lagos, Nigeria
- **Established:** 1998
- **Annual Revenue:** ₦15.2 billion ($33 million USD)
- **Total Employees:** 850 staff across 3 locations (Lagos, Kaduna, Port Harcourt)
- **Industry:** Automotive parts manufacturing and industrial equipment production
- **Key Clients:** Major automotive manufacturers, industrial conglomerates, and export markets in Europe and Asia

**Organizational Structure:**
- **Board of Directors:** 8 members (CEO, CFO, COO, CTO, and 4 external directors)
- **IT Department:** 12 staff (1 IT Manager, 2 Network Administrators, 3 System Administrators, 2 Database Administrators, 2 Help Desk Support, 2 Junior IT Support)
- **Operations:** 450 staff
- **Sales & Marketing:** 120 staff
- **Finance & Administration:** 180 staff
- **Research & Development:** 100 staff

**Current IT Infrastructure:**
- 600+ workstations and laptops
- 8 physical servers (on-premises data center)
- 3 branch office networks
- Email system with 850+ users
- ERP system (SAP) managing production, inventory, and financial data
- Manufacturing control systems (MES - Manufacturing Execution System)
- Customer relationship management (CRM) system
- Minimal cloud infrastructure (only file backup service)

#### The Ransomware Attack Incident

**Attack Timeline:**
- **Date:** March 15, 2026
- **Discovery:** March 17, 2026 (2 days after initial infection)
- **Duration of Downtime:** 5 days (March 17-22, 2026)
- **Attack Vector:** Phishing email with malicious attachment sent to 3 employees in the Finance department
- **Ransomware Variant:** LockBit 3.0
- **Ransom Demand:** $500,000 USD
- **Data Exfiltrated:** Estimated 50GB of sensitive data (customer lists, financial records, product designs)

**Impact Assessment:**
- **Financial Loss:** ₦45 million ($98,000 USD) in direct costs:
  - ₦25 million in production downtime and lost revenue
  - ₦12 million in incident response and recovery costs
  - ₦8 million in ransom payment (company decided to pay)
- **Operational Impact:** 5-day production halt affecting 15 major orders
- **Reputational Damage:** 3 major clients threatened contract termination
- **Data Breach:** Customer data and proprietary designs exposed
- **Regulatory Exposure:** Potential NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) violations

**Root Causes Identified:**
- No email security gateway or advanced threat protection
- Lack of employee security awareness training
- No endpoint detection and response (EDR) solution
- Weak password policies (no multi-factor authentication)
- No network segmentation between departments
- Outdated antivirus software (last updated 6 months prior)
- No backup and disaster recovery plan in place
- Limited logging and monitoring capabilities
- No incident response plan or security team

#### Current Security Posture Assessment

**Critical Vulnerabilities:**

1. **Network Security:**
   - No firewall with intrusion detection/prevention capabilities
   - No network segmentation (all systems on flat network)
   - No VPN for remote workers
   - Wireless network uses WPA2 with weak password
   - No network access control (NAC)

2. **Endpoint Security:**
   - Outdated antivirus on only 60% of workstations
   - No mobile device management (MDM)
   - No endpoint detection and response (EDR)
   - Unpatched systems (average 45 days behind on patches)
   - USB ports not restricted

3. **Access Control:**
   - No multi-factor authentication (MFA)
   - Weak password policy (minimum 6 characters)
   - Shared administrative credentials
   - No role-based access control (RBAC)
   - No privileged access management (PAM)

4. **Data Protection:**
   - No data encryption at rest or in transit
   - No data loss prevention (DLP) tools
   - Sensitive data stored on shared drives with minimal access controls
   - No backup encryption
   - Backups stored on-site (vulnerable to ransomware)

5. **Incident Response & Monitoring:**
   - No Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)
   - No 24/7 security monitoring
   - No incident response plan
   - Limited logging capabilities
   - No security operations center (SOC)

6. **Compliance & Governance:**
   - No formal security policy documentation
   - No security governance framework
   - No regular security audits or penetration testing
   - No compliance with NDPR requirements
   - No data protection officer (DPO) appointed

**Your Findings & Assessment:**

- The company's security posture is **critically weak** and poses significant business risk.
- The company is **not compliant** with industry best practices (ISO 27001, NIST Cybersecurity Framework) and **violates NDPR requirements**.
- The company's employees have **not received any formal security awareness training** in the past 3 years.
- The current IT infrastructure is **outdated** and lacks modern security controls.
- The company has **no dedicated security personnel** or security team.
- **Immediate action is required** to prevent future attacks and protect critical business assets.

#### Financial Context for Board Presentation

**Current IT Budget:** ₦120 million annually (₦10 million/month)
- 70% allocated to salaries and maintenance
- 30% available for new investments and improvements
- **Available budget for security improvements:** ₦36 million (₦3 million/month)

**Business Impact of Future Attacks:**
- Estimated annual revenue at risk: ₦2.5 billion (if major production outage occurs)
- Potential regulatory fines under NDPR: Up to ₦50 million
- Customer contract penalties: ₦500 million+ (loss of major clients)
- Brand reputation damage: Immeasurable

**Board Priorities:**
- Prevent future ransomware attacks
- Ensure business continuity and operational resilience
- Protect customer data and comply with regulations
- Minimize downtime and financial losses
- Maintain customer trust and market position

### Your Task

1. **Prepare a 10-minute group presentation:** The presentation should be targeted at the Board of Directors and should be clear, concise, and persuasive. The presentation should include the following:
    - An overview of the current state of security.
    - A summary of the key risks and vulnerabilities.
    - A proposed set of security investments, including a cost-benefit analysis.
    - A call to action.
2. **Create a slide deck:** The slide deck should be professional and well-designed. It should use visuals to help communicate your message. All group members must contribute to the presentation.
